# Photos: what the app stores ask for

> **What this is:** the App Store and Google Play rules that apply to photos, and how MeshTrax meets each one, so a submission with photos has its answers ready. How photos work is in [technical/photos.md](../technical/photos.md).
>
> **Status:** checked on 2026-11-22 against:
> - Apple's App Review Guidelines (last updated October 16, 2026);
> - Google Play's Developer Program Policy (effective October 29, 2026), its User Generated Content and AI-Generated Content pages, and the Data safety help page.
>
> These change; re-read the pages linked below before each submission.

## Why photos need this

Photos are content users share, so the stores' user-generated content rules apply, as they do to messages. They also involve AI:
- the receiving phone rebuilds each picture with a generative model;
- **Enhance 4×** enlarges it with another;
- **Subject only** and **Auto crop** outline what the sender taps with a small on-device model (MediaPipe's Interactive Segmenter). It finds an outline and generates nothing; the sender's photo is cropped to it or blacked out around it.

Apple's guideline 1.2 names "AI content creation apps". Google's AI-generated content policy covers an "image or video generated by AI based on text, image, or voice prompts". Treat both as applying.

## App Store

| Guideline | What it asks | How photos meet it |
|---|---|---|
| [1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content) User-generated content | A way to filter objectionable material from being posted | The on-device safety classifier checks every photo before it's coded and sent, and every rebuilt and enhanced picture before it's shown, on both phones. A flagged picture is hidden and never stored. |
| | A way to report offensive content, and timely responses | **Report** in the photo's ⋮ menu, full screen: the photo is hidden at once, and a report goes from inside the app to the report inbox, which emails the developer. The developer answers from the published address. |
| | The ability to block abusive users | **Block** in the same menu. A blocked sender's photos are dropped, not kept. |
| | Published contact information | In the terms, the privacy policy and the listing. |
| 1.2 (October 16, 2026) | Prevent and remove nonconsensual intimate imagery, including imagery made with AI | No text-to-image and no prompts: the model only rebuilds what the sender photographed. The only edits are the sender's own: a crop, blurred faces, and the black around what they tapped for Subject only. Nudity is refused before sending and hidden before showing. The terms forbid such imagery by name. |
| 1.1.2, 1.1.4 | No realistic violence or sexual material | The classifier's NSFL and NSFW thresholds, on originals, rebuilt pictures and enhanced pictures. |
| 2.5.19 | No hidden features, nothing that changes after review | The review notes describe the AI features: the rebuild, Enhance 4× and the outline for Subject only and Auto crop. Nothing turns on after approval. |
| 5.1.1 | Use the system picker; purpose strings say what access is for | Photos come through the system photo picker. Saving asks only to add to the library (`NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`). The camera and photo library strings mention photos. |

**Age rating.**
- The capabilities are Messaging and User-Generated Content, as for messages; photos don't add one.
- Separately, for the whole app: since November 16, 2026 Apple asks about Shared Location and Dating, due by January 12, 2027. MeshTrax shares positions, so Shared Location is yes, and Dating is no.

**App Privacy label.**
- Apple's "collect" means sent off the device where the developer or its partners can read it. A photo sent over the mesh is never readable by the developer, so declare it as messages are declared.
- A photo report does reach the developer. Declare it, in the same way as message reports: User Content (Photos or Videos, for the photo's code when included, and Other User Content, for the reason), not linked to the user, not used for tracking, for App Functionality.

**Review notes and video.**
- The notes in [app-review-notes.md](../app-review-notes.md) say what the AI features are and how a reviewer can see them. A photo needs two radios, so the demo video shows one being sent, rebuilt, enhanced, saved and reported.

## Google Play

| Policy | What it asks | How photos meet it |
|---|---|---|
| [User Generated Content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937) | Users accept the terms before they can create or upload content | The first-launch terms screen (`TermsGate`), before anything can be sent. |
| | The terms define objectionable content and forbid it | [terms.md](../terms.md), section 2 and "Zero tolerance". |
| | An in-app system to report content and users, and to block users | **Report** and **Block** in the photo's menu, from inside the app. |
| | Act on reports | The developer reads the inbox, and improves blocking and filtering. |
| | Incidental sexual content only behind filters | None is allowed: it is refused, not filtered. |
| [AI-Generated Content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13985936) | Prevent the generation of restricted content | The classifier on every rebuilt and enhanced picture, and on every photo before it's sent, a Subject only one as it will be sent. There's no prompt to misuse. |
| | Users can report offensive content to the developer **without needing to exit the app** | The report is sent from inside the app to the report inbox. |
| | Use reports to improve filtering | Reports reach the developer; filter changes ship as a new model version (see [technical/photos.md](../technical/photos.md)). |
| Generative AI review guidance (August 2026) | Reviewers can use the AI features, and safety tests are documented | The review notes and video. Where the checks run, and their thresholds, are in technical/photos.md. MeshTrax never tests with explicit material; its safety rests on the classifier's own published evaluation and the thresholds above. |

**Data safety.**
- **Photos** (Photos and videos → Photos): collected, since a photo leaves the device over the mesh, and on Public the channel key is public, so it isn't end-to-end in Google's sense. Not shared: the user starts the transfer to other users. Optional; App functionality; encrypted in transit, as for messages.
- **Photo reports:** collected, optional, App functionality. Photos for the code when included, and Other user-generated content for the reason. Declare message reports the same way once they use the inbox.

**Permissions.**
- No media-read permission: photos come through the system photo picker.
- Save writes through MediaStore, which needs no permission on Android 10 and later.
- `CAMERA` was already declared, for QR codes.

**Child safety standards** apply only to apps in the Social or Dating categories. Check the category before each submission.

## Before each submission with photos

1. Re-read Apple's 1.2 and Google's User Generated Content and AI-Generated Content pages; note the dates here.
2. Send a test report and check it arrives at the published address.
3. Update the review notes and the demo video if the photo screens changed.
4. Answer the age rating, App Privacy and Data safety forms as above.
